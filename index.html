From Tundikhel to the big pitch

मेरो कथा

I was born and raised in Kathmandu. My first ball was a plastic one my father bought from a stall in Asan, and my first pitch was any open patch of ground we could find before the evening traffic came. On weekends my cousins and I walked to Tundikhel and played until the light went.

I grew up watching Brazil's number tens on a small television at a neighbour's house. I loved how they played with their heads up and never looked scared of the defender in front of them. I spent hours in our lane trying the step-overs, the elasticos and the no-look passes, and broke more than one window doing it.

Futsal courts in the valley taught me close control. School football taught me to run for the team. Academy football taught me that talent without discipline goes nowhere. I still carry all three lessons onto the pitch.
